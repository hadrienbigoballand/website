Optimal Steps for Fast Diffeomorphic Shape Registration

Hadrien Bigo-Balland1, Tom Boeken1,2, Jean Feydy1

1 Inria, Université Paris Cité, Inserm, HeKA, Paris    2 Hôpital Européen Georges Pompidou, AP-HP, Paris

Deep learning performs well on registration tasks, with fast inference. But three constraints limit its use in clinical studies: it requires a training corpus, which small clinical cohorts rarely provide; its accuracy degrades on anatomical variations absent from the training set; and each new anatomy carries a substantial retraining cost. Optimization-based methods are free of all three, but they remain substantially slower. That is the gap we address, with ideas drawn from the computer vision literature: a learning-free registration model that is diffeomorphic and roughly forty times faster than the geometric state of the art. The code will ship in scikit-shapes under an MIT licence.
